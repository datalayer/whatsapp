## Each application's own Meta app

- **Onboarding other businesses is a partner's path, not Datalayer's.** To provide WhatsApp messaging to businesses it onboards (through Embedded Signup), a company becomes a Tech Provider — its business verified, App Review (two videos: a message sent and received, a template created), Advanced Access to `whatsapp_business_messaging` and `whatsapp_business_management` — or works with a Solution Partner. A developer using their own app on their own account needs none of this: hence one Meta app per application, and no Meta app of Datalayer's. Source: https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/business-messaging/whatsapp/solution-providers/get-started-for-tech-providers — read 24 October 2026.
- **Pre-verified numbers** are offered by Solution Partners only (bought and verified in advance, claimed within 90 days of their verification or back to unverified, a line of credit needed): the later step, should Datalayer supply numbers. Source: https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/business-messaging/whatsapp/solution-providers/pre-verified-phone-numbers — the page did not render for a reader on 24 October 2026; re-read before relying on it.
- **A system user token for one's own app.** In the business's settings, a system user is given the app and the WhatsApp business account, then a token is generated for the app with `whatsapp_business_messaging` and `whatsapp_business_management`; the temporary token of the app's API setup page expires after 24 hours and is not for this. `GET /debug_token?input_token=<token>` with the app's own token (`<app_id>|<app_secret>`) answers `data.app_id`, `data.is_valid`, `data.scopes` and `data.granular_scopes[].target_ids` Read in Meta's Solution Partner guide (its _system user_ steps) and integration guides, 24 October 2026; re-read Meta's access-token page before relying on it.
- **The app's webhook.** The app's WhatsApp → Configuration takes a callback URL and a verify token, checked when saved (`GET ?hub.mode=subscribe&hub.verify_token=…&hub.challenge=…`); the fields are subscribed there; each POST is signed `X-Hub-Signature-256` with that app's secret. `POST /<waba_id>/subscribed_apps` with the system user token subscribes that token's app to the account. Each installation has its own address (`src/webhook.ts`): every app subscribed to a business account gets that account's webhooks, so several applications on one account each get them, and a message is claimed once.
- **A number registered already.** Registering takes the number's two-step verification PIN when one is set (the PIN given otherwise becomes it); register and deregister are each limited to 10 requests per number in a moving 72 hours (`133016`, then 72 hours blocked). Source: https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/business-messaging/whatsapp/business-phone-numbers/registration — read 24 October 2026. A wrong PIN is answered `133005` (PIN mismatch), and a number's `platform_type` is `CLOUD_API` once it is on the Cloud API: both from Meta's error reference and phone-number listings as quoted by integrators, 24 October 2026; re-read before relying on them. What `src/install.ts` does: a number on the Cloud API already is not registered again; otherwise it is registered with the PIN the business gave, else a new one; `133005` is said in its own sentence.

## Groups and channels

- **Groups (Groups API).** Only an Official Business Account's numbers; not for numbers on the WhatsApp Business app, nor numbers onboarded to Multi-solution Conversations. Invite-only (each participant is sent an invite link); at most 8 participants a group, at most 10,000 groups a business number, at most one Cloud API business a group. Text, media and text- or media-based templates; no calling, interactive messages, disappearing or view-once messages, edits or deletes. Source: https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/business-messaging/whatsapp/groups — read 25 October 2026. Not built: each application's groups would be its own number's, through its own app.
- **Channels** are WhatsApp's one-way broadcast product: followers do not write in a channel, so an application cannot answer there. Not a Cloud API conversation.

## Prices

- Each business number has a free tier of **1,000 delivered service messages a month**, not carried over. From **1 October 2026** Meta charges again for service messages past it and for utility templates sent inside the window, and updated 40 rates (9 markets out of the regional groups). Source: https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/business-messaging/whatsapp/pricing — read 23 October 2026.

- **AI Providers.** General-purpose AI assistants are permitted on the platform only where Meta is legally required to permit them: Italy (from 16 February 2026), the EEA markets listed (from 11 March 2026), both free from 13 May 2026; Brazil (from 11 March 2026, charged); Türkiye (from 15 October 2026, free). The page and its rate files were updated 15 October 2026; the terms it cites are of 15 January 2026. A business serving its own customers and team with an application is not this case. Source: https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/business-messaging/whatsapp/pricing/ai-providers — read 23 October 2026.

Source: https://whatsappbusiness.com/policy/ — read 23 October 2026.

## The Personal Agent Protocol and Meta

Read 23 October 2026.

- **What it is.** Sierra and Meta announced the Personal Agent Protocol ("Poppy") on 6 October 2026: how a person's own agent signs in to, and acts with, a company. Draft 0.2 is dated 18 October 2026. Sources: https://thepaypers.com/payments/news/meta-and-sierra-develop-personal-agent-protocol-for-ai-agents (9 October 2026); https://apievangelist.com/2026/10/19/the-first-poppy-files-and-the-one-a-vendor-wrote/ (19 October 2026).
- **Meta's stated use.** Its own consumer agent, **Muse**: "Personal Agent Protocol is designed to enable agents like Meta's Muse work with businesses to get things done" (https://constellationr.com/insights/news/meta-sierra-pitch-personal-agent-protocol-govern-how-personal-ai-agents-deal-business, 7 October 2026). Nothing published says Muse, Meta AI or Meta Business Agent speaks the protocol inside WhatsApp, nor that Muse reaches a business's number through it.
- **WhatsApp's side.** Neither the Cloud API, the WhatsApp Business Account nor Business Manager names a protocol company: a search of Meta's WhatsApp developer documentation and of the coverage found no field, endpoint or setting that refers to the protocol.
- **Discovery.** Draft 0.2's `/.well-known/poppy.json` has `protocol_version`, `organization`, `auth`, `agent` (`agent.protocols[]`: `type`, an https `endpoint`, optional `resource`), `apis`, `web`, `feedback` and `extensions`; **no field for a messaging channel** (WhatsApp, SMS, a phone number), and its hand-off is to a person at the company within the same conversation. Source: https://personalagentprotocol.org/docs/0.2/spec. No launch partner served a `poppy.json` on 19 October 2026 (API Evangelist, above).
- **What follows here.** An application's protocol door (its web address) and its WhatsApp number stay two separate doors to the same application: nothing in either standard ties them. Meta's own agent reaching an application through the protocol, or a conversation handed from the protocol to a WhatsApp chat, waits until Meta publishes it.
