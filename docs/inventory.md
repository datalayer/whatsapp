## Each application's own Meta app

- **Onboarding other businesses is a partner's path, not Datalayer's.** To provide WhatsApp messaging to businesses it onboards (through Embedded Signup), a company becomes a Tech Provider — its business verified, App Review (two videos: a message sent and received, a template created), Advanced Access to `whatsapp_business_messaging` and `whatsapp_business_management` — or works with a Solution Partner. A developer using their own app on their own account needs none of this: hence one Meta app per application, and no Meta app of Datalayer's. Source: https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/business-messaging/whatsapp/solution-providers/get-started-for-tech-providers — read 24 October 2026.
- **Pre-verified numbers** are offered by Solution Partners only (bought and verified in advance, claimed within 90 days of their verification or back to unverified, a line of credit needed): the later step, should Datalayer supply numbers. Source: https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/business-messaging/whatsapp/solution-providers/pre-verified-phone-numbers — the page did not render for a reader on 24 October 2026; re-read before relying on it.
- **A system user token for one's own app.** In the business's settings, a system user is given the app and the WhatsApp business account, then a token is generated for the app with `whatsapp_business_messaging` and `whatsapp_business_management`; the temporary token of the app's API setup page expires after 24 hours and is not for this. `GET /debug_token?input_token=<token>` with the app's own token (`<app_id>|<app_secret>`) answers `data.app_id`, `data.is_valid`, `data.scopes` and `data.granular_scopes[].target_ids` Read in Meta's Solution Partner guide (its _system user_ steps) and integration guides, 24 October 2026; re-read Meta's access-token page before relying on it.
- **The app's webhook.** The app's WhatsApp → Configuration takes a callback URL and a verify token, checked when saved (`GET ?hub.mode=subscribe&hub.verify_token=…&hub.challenge=…`); the fields are subscribed there; each POST is signed `X-Hub-Signature-256` with that app's secret. `POST /<waba_id>/subscribed_apps` with the system user token subscribes that token's app to the account. Each installation has its own address (`src/webhook.ts`): every app subscribed to a business account gets that account's webhooks, so several applications on one account each get them, and a message is claimed once.
- **A number registered already.** Registering takes the number's two-step verification PIN when one is set (the PIN given otherwise becomes it); register and deregister are each limited to 10 requests per number in a moving 72 hours (`133016`, then 72 hours blocked). Source: https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/business-messaging/whatsapp/business-phone-numbers/registration — read 24 October 2026. A wrong PIN is answered `133005` (PIN mismatch), and a number's `platform_type` is `CLOUD_API` once it is on the Cloud API: both from Meta's error reference and phone-number listings as quoted by integrators, 24 October 2026; re-read before relying on them. What `src/install.ts` does: a number on the Cloud API already is not registered again; otherwise it is registered with the PIN the business gave, else a new one; `133005` is said in its own sentence.

## Groups and channels

- **Groups (Groups API).** Only an Official Business Account's numbers; not for numbers on the WhatsApp Business app, nor numbers onboarded to Multi-solution Conversations. Invite-only (each participant is sent an invite link); at most 8 participants a group, at most 10,000 groups a business number, at most one Cloud API business a group. Text, media and text- or media-based templates; no calling, interactive messages, disappearing or view-once messages, auth or commerce messages, edits or deletes. Per-message pricing: a billable message is charged once per participant it is delivered to; a message from anyone in a group opens a customer service window with the whole group, and from 1 October 2026 a group's free-form messages are free only within the number's 1,000-message free tier, shared with 1:1 and counted once per delivered participant. Sources: https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/business-messaging/whatsapp/groups and its `/pricing` page — read 25 October 2026.
- **The Groups API's calls** (Group management reference, read 25 October 2026): `POST /<phone_number_id>/groups` `{messaging_product, subject (≤ 128), description? (≤ 2,048), join_approval_mode? (auto_approve by default)}` — its synchronous answer is not documented; the `group_lifecycle_update` webhook (`groups[].type` `group_create`, with `group_id`, `request_id`, `subject`, `invite_link`, or `errors`) answers it. `GET /<phone_number_id>/groups` (`limit` ≤ 1,024) lists `data.groups[] {id, subject, created_at}`. `GET` and `POST /<group_id>/invite_link` read or reset the link (`https://chat.whatsapp.com/<id>`; a reset kills every older link). `DELETE /<group_id>` deletes the group and removes everyone, the business too. `GET /<group_id>?fields=subject,participants,…`, join requests (`/<group_id>/join_requests`), removing participants (a removed person cannot rejoin by the link) and settings exist and are not used. Messages: `POST /<phone_number_id>/messages` with `recipient_type: "group"` and `to: <group_id>`; a received group message carries `group_id`, and its sender in `from` and the contact (Meta's group pages show the phone number and `wa_id` there and do not show `from_user_id`: the ingress reads the BSUID as for a 1:1 message and drops a message without one); a status names the group in `recipient_id` with `recipient_type: "group"`; no mention syntax is documented. Webhook fields to subscribe: `group_lifecycle_update`, `group_participants_update`, `group_settings_update`, `group_status_update` (`group_suspend`, `group_suspend_cleared`). Errors: `131215` the number not eligible, `131211` 10,000 groups made, `131214` creation paused after too many marketing messages, `131208` too many group operations, `131041` group unknown, `131207` group suspended. Sources: https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/business-messaging/whatsapp/groups/reference, `/groups/groups-messaging`, `/groups/webhooks`, `/groups/error-codes`, `/webhooks/reference/messages/group` — read 25 October 2026. Group ids seen in Meta's examples are base64 (`Y2FwaV9ncm91cDo…`, `capi_group:<number>:<id>`): they may carry the business's own number, never a participant's.
- **Official Business Account.** A business phone number verified as an authentic business (the blue check). Conditions: the WhatsApp Business Messaging Policy; registered on the WhatsApp Business Platform for at least 30 days; the business portfolio's Business Verification; two-step verification on the number; its display name approved. Not granted to employees, test accounts or WhatsApp Business app numbers; a denied request may be made again after 30 days. Requested in WhatsApp Manager (Overview → the number → Profile → Official business account → Submit Request) or by API. `GET /<phone_number_id>/official_business_account` (fields `oba_status`, `status_message`; or `GET /<phone_number_id>?fields=official_business_account`) answers `oba_status`: `NOT_STARTED` in the guide's example; the reference's values `PENDING`, `APPROVED`, `REJECTED`, `UNDER_REVIEW`, `EXPIRED`, `CANCELLED`. `POST /<phone_number_id>/official_business_account` submits, resubmits after a rejection, or withdraws: `business_website_url` and `primary_country_of_operation` required; `primary_language`, `parent_business_or_brand`, `supporting_links` (5 to 10) and `additional_supporting_information` optional; answers `{success, message, updated_status?, tracking_id?}`. What the service reads of the conditions: `name_status` on the number (`APPROVED`, `AVAILABLE_WITHOUT_REVIEW`, `DECLINED`, `EXPIRED`, `PENDING_REVIEW`, `NONE`; in beta, "not available to all developers"), `business_verification_status` on the business account (`VERIFIED`, `PENDING`, `NOT_VERIFIED`, `REJECTED`…; the example answers lowercase `verified`); Meta gives no registration date and no two-step verification flag (the reference's `code_verification_status` is the number's code verification). Sources: https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/business-messaging/whatsapp/official-business-accounts, `/reference/whatsapp-business-phone-number/whatsapp-business-account-official-business-account-status-api`, `/reference/whatsapp-business-phone-number/whatsapp-business-account-phone-number-api`, `/business-phone-numbers/phone-numbers`, `/whatsapp-business-accounts` — read 25 October 2026.
- **What `src/groups.ts` and `src/manage.ts` do with it**: the card reads the status and the conditions and requests it through the API; groups are created from the card once it is `APPROVED`; nothing of a group's participants and no invite link is kept.
- **Channels** are WhatsApp's one-way broadcast product: followers do not write in a channel, so an application cannot answer there. Not a Cloud API conversation.

## Prices

- Each business number has a free tier of **1,000 delivered service messages a month**, not carried over. From **1 October 2026** Meta charges again for service messages past it and for utility templates sent inside the window, and updated 40 rates (9 markets out of the regional groups). Rates go by the recipient's country code; **Belgium (+32) is in "Rest of Western Europe"**, its rate in Meta's rate-card files. Source: https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/business-messaging/whatsapp/pricing — read 23 October 2026.

## Terms and policy

- **AI Providers.** General-purpose AI assistants are permitted on the platform only where Meta is legally required to permit them: Italy (from 16 February 2026), the EEA markets listed (from 11 March 2026), both free from 13 May 2026; Brazil (from 11 March 2026, charged); Türkiye (from 15 October 2026, free). The page and its rate files were updated 15 October 2026; the terms it cites are of 15 January 2026. A business serving its own customers and team with an application is not this case. Source: https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/business-messaging/whatsapp/pricing/ai-providers — read 23 October 2026.
- **WhatsApp Business Messaging Policy**, last updated 23 September 2026: automation is allowed inside the 24-hour window with "prompt, clear, and direct escalation paths" (a person in the chat, a phone number, email, web support, a store, a form); the policy does not mention AI. Source: https://whatsappbusiness.com/policy/ — read 23 October 2026.

## The Personal Agent Protocol and Meta

Read 23 October 2026.

- **What it is.** Sierra and Meta announced the Personal Agent Protocol ("Poppy") on 6 October 2026: how a person's own agent signs in to, and acts with, a company. Draft 0.2 is dated 18 October 2026. Sources: https://thepaypers.com/payments/news/meta-and-sierra-develop-personal-agent-protocol-for-ai-agents (9 October 2026); https://apievangelist.com/2026/10/19/the-first-poppy-files-and-the-one-a-vendor-wrote/ (19 October 2026).
- **Meta's stated use.** Its own consumer agent, **Muse**: "Personal Agent Protocol is designed to enable agents like Meta's Muse work with businesses to get things done" (https://constellationr.com/insights/news/meta-sierra-pitch-personal-agent-protocol-govern-how-personal-ai-agents-deal-business, 7 October 2026). Nothing published says Muse, Meta AI or Meta Business Agent speaks the protocol inside WhatsApp, nor that Muse reaches a business's number through it.
- **WhatsApp's side.** Neither the Cloud API, the WhatsApp Business Account nor Business Manager names a protocol company: a search of Meta's WhatsApp developer documentation and of the coverage found no field, endpoint or setting that refers to the protocol.
- **Discovery.** Draft 0.2's `/.well-known/poppy.json` has `protocol_version`, `organization`, `auth`, `agent` (`agent.protocols[]`: `type`, an https `endpoint`, optional `resource`), `apis`, `web`, `feedback` and `extensions`; **no field for a messaging channel** (WhatsApp, SMS, a phone number), and its hand-off is to a person at the company within the same conversation. Source: https://personalagentprotocol.org/docs/0.2/spec. No launch partner served a `poppy.json` on 19 October 2026 (API Evangelist, above).
- **What follows here.** An application's protocol door (its web address) and its WhatsApp number stay two separate doors to the same application: nothing in either standard ties them. Meta's own agent reaching an application through the protocol, or a conversation handed from the protocol to a WhatsApp chat, waits until Meta publishes it.
